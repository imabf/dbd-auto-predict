# Streamer DMs (for channels with ~50-300 viewers)

Rules: one DM per person, no follow-up spam. If they don't reply, leave it. Check their panels or rules for how they prefer to be contacted (some say "no DMs", so use the channel they list). Replace [brackets].

## Variant A: short and direct

Hi [name], I'm a solo student developer and I enjoy your DBD streams. I'm thinking about building a free tool that spots the end-of-match screen and resolves your Twitch Prediction automatically. It isn't built yet. Could I get 2 minutes of feedback on whether it'd be useful? No pressure at all if not. Thanks either way!

## Variant B: ask about their workflow

Hey [name], solo student dev here. Quick question for a project I'm validating: when you run Predictions during DBD, how do you handle resolving them between matches? I'm exploring a small app that reads the results screen (like OBS reads your screen, nothing touching game files) and does it for you. Nothing's built yet. If you've got 2 minutes to tell me what would or wouldn't work for you, I'd really appreciate it. Totally fine to ignore this.

## Variant C: lead with the page

Hi [name]! I'm a student developer working on an idea for DBD streamers: automatic Prediction resolution plus a small stats overlay (escape rate, streak). It's still just an idea, and I put up a waitlist page to see if anyone wants it: https://imabf.github.io/dbd-auto-predict/. If you have 2 minutes to tell me honestly what you think, that'd help a lot. No worries if you're busy.
