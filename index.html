How it would work

  1. 1

    It watches your screen

    Like OBS does. It looks at the picture only. No game memory, no game files.

  2. 2

    It spots the result

    When the end-of-match screen shows up, it reads whether you escaped, died or got sacrificed.

  3. 3

    It resolves your Prediction

    Your Twitch Prediction gets resolved and a stats overlay updates. You keep playing.

Who it's for

Small and mid-size Dead by Daylight streamers, especially Affiliates who run Predictions and are tired of juggling them mid-match. Viewers and mods can join the list too, so we know who's interested.

Questions

Does it touch game files?

No. It doesn't read game memory or touch game files. It only looks at your screen.

Does it work without Affiliate?

Twitch Predictions need Twitch Affiliate or Partner, so for the Prediction part, you need that. The stats overlay idea doesn't depend on Predictions.

How much will it cost?

The plan is a free basic tier, and a paid tier for stats history and themes. Nothing is final.

Is it available now?

No. It's in development, and whether we build it depends on how many people want it.
